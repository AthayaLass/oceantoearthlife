The Creation of Veranaturae

Veranaturae means "true nature" - the essence of who we are beneath the noise, the rush, and the roles we play. Through mindfulness, yoga, and Ayurveda, each person can gently return to their own inner home: a place of clarity, compassion, and serenity.

Athelas Diving - Professional Diving Services

Our name draws inspiration from Athelas, the legendary "King's Herb" from Middle-earth. Like this mystical plant with extraordinary healing properties, diving transports us to an otherworldly underwater universe where we discover nature's magic and find inner healing beneath the waves.

Helping Dreissena System Fight Invasive Mussels

We're partnering with Dreissena System to combat invasive mussel species in Swiss lakes. Our underwater expertise is helping to protect these precious aquatic ecosystems and preserve the natural balance of our local waters.

Underwater Mindfulness - Project in Development

Experience the ultimate connection with nature through guided underwater meditation sessions. This innovative project combines the serenity of underwater environments with mindfulness practices, creating a unique wellness experience that connects ocean, earth, and life.
